# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Bootstrap 5 + Modern Vanilla JavaScript + Custom Monochromatic CSS (Zero-runtime overhead, high performance)

## Users

Streetwear enthusiasts, athletes, and minimalist design purists who wear oversized heavyweight apparel for daily life, travel, and training. Individuals who seek heavyweight organic cotton, architectural drop-shoulder cuts, clean drape, and premium durability stripped of loud graphics, neon trims, or synthetic gimmicks.

## Product Purpose

Deliver a modern, natural, distraction-free streetwear shopping experience combining Gymshark's seamless conversion mechanics (hover quick-size selectors, slide-over bag drawer, real-time inventory and shipping tracker) with the architectural silhouette and fabric discipline of luxury streetwear (Represent, Fear of God, Cole Buxton).

## Positioning

The convergence of science-based lifting protocols and everyday luxury streetwear. SUPERSCIENCE provides evidence-based training programs (hypertrophy volume landmarks, RIR/RPE tracking, lengthened-partial overload) alongside heavyweight apparel (380–450 GSM oversized boxy tees, relaxed French terry hoodies, and wide-leg bottoms) engineered to support heavy lifting and daily life.

## Operating Context

Desktop and mobile web browsers. Lifters and shoppers discovering new seasonal drops, exploring evidence-based workout programs (Upper/Lower, PPL, Powerbuilding), reviewing exercise biomechanics, selecting apparel sizes via instant 1-click quick-size drawers, and checking out seamlessly.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Framework: Bootstrap 5 responsive grid system, offcanvas navigation and cart, modals, accordions, and toasts.
- Visual Constraints: Pure minimalist monochrome palette (Stark White `#FFFFFF`, Clean Studio Neutral `#F8F8F9`, Pitch Black `#000000`, Subtle Hairline `#E5E5E7`). Strict 0px border-radius across all buttons, cards, and drawers.
- Initial Launch Scope (Separated Multi-Page Architecture): 
  1. `index.html` — **Home Page**: High-impact hero banner, category spotlights (T-Shirts, Shorts, Caps), featured workout protocols teaser, curated Drop 01 bestseller showcase, and studio showroom teaser.
  2. `catalog.html` — **Garment Collection / Shop All**: Full 12-specimen authentic product catalog with dynamic category filtering (T-Shirts, Shorts, Caps), sort selector, 2-col vs 4-col grid toggle, quick-size drawers (`S | M | L | XL`), and quick-view PDP modals.
  3. `programs.html` — **Science-Based Training Programs Hub**: Complete hypertrophy protocols (4-Day Upper/Lower, 6-Day PPL, 5-Day Powerbuilding, 3-Day Full Body), volume landmarks matrix, and full exercise tables with coaching cues.
  4. `contact.html` — **Customer Care & Showroom**: Personalized sizing consultation form, private showroom fitting appointment scheduler, flagship studio coordinates, and comprehensive interactive FAQ accordion.
  5. `profile.html` — **Athlete Vault & Profile**: Order history tracking, persistent saved specimens wishlist, and profile metrics.
- Key Interactions: 
  - Gymshark-style slide-up quick-size selector (`S | M | L | XL`) on product cards with instant 1-click add to bag.
  - Slide-over bag drawer (`offcanvas-end`) with real-time Free Worldwide Express Shipping progress meter (₱8,500 threshold) and promo code engine (`SCIENCE10`, `DROP01`).
  - Interactive Workout Program Modal with day-by-day routines, exercise tables, sets x reps, RIR intensity, and printable protocol sheet.
  - 2-col/4-col responsive catalog grid view switcher.

## Brand Commitments

- Brandmark: Bold, slanted uppercase SUPERSCIENCE logo (`assets/logo.jpg`).
- Voice & Tone: Technical, authoritative, minimal, focused on human biomechanics and fabric science.
- Anti-Slop Directive: Reject generic AI aesthetics—no rounded cookie-cutter buttons, no rainbow gradients, no uncalibrated spacing, no boilerplate cards.

## Product Principles

1. Performance First: Every interaction must feel immediate, tactile, and frictionless—from instant card hover states to the slide-over bag drawer.
2. Monochromatic Discipline: Color is never used for mere decoration. High contrast, sharp negative space, razor-thin hairline borders, and typographic hierarchy define the visual world.
3. Spec-Driven Honesty: Product details emphasize real performance metrics (fabric GSM weight, fiber composition, stretch/recovery properties, thermal breathability).
4. Responsive Integrity: Flawless mobile-first responsiveness (smooth 2-column mobile catalog with thumb-friendly controls) scaling up to a dense 4-column desktop experience.
